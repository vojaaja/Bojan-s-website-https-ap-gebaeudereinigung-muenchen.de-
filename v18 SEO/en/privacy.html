2. What data we collect

We only collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us — specifically, when you fill out our contact form. This includes: your name, company name (optional), email address, phone number, property type, and the message you write.

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking scripts on this website.

3. Why we collect it (legal basis)

We process this data solely to respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, to prepare a quote or carry out a contract with you. This is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (processing necessary for pre-contractual measures or performance of a contract) and, where no contract results, on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries sent to us).

4. Contact form — Formspree

Our contact form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. When you submit the form, the data you enter is transmitted to and stored by Formspree's servers before being forwarded to us by email. Formspree may process data outside the EU/EEA. You can find more information in Formspree's own privacy policy.

5. Web fonts — Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts (Archivo, IBM Plex Sans) to display text consistently. These fonts are loaded from Google's servers when you visit our site. As a result, your device's IP address is transmitted to Google. Google may process this data in the United States. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

6. How long we keep your data

We retain the information you submit through the contact form only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and, where a business relationship follows, for as long as required by German commercial and tax law.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ap.gebaudereinigungmunich@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in Bavaria, this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).
