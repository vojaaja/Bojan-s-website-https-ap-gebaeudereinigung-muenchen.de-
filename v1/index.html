Reliable cleaning, built on experience.

AP Gebäudereinigung is a Munich-based cleaning company providing professional building maintenance for businesses, property managers, and private clients across the city and surrounding areas.

We specialize in thorough, dependable cleaning — from daily office upkeep to large-scale machine cleaning of industrial and commercial facilities. Whether it's a single office or a large industrial site, we tailor our approach to fit the specific needs of each building.
